## 🔭 Why Spyglass?

**Spyglass** lets you look through a spyglass whenever you want, without carrying one. Hold a key and you get the real vanilla spyglass experience: the smooth zoom, the sounds, and your character raising the spyglass to their eye.

**No new items, no new mechanics, just a better way to look far away.**

- 🎒 **No item needed**: hold **C** to zoom, even with empty hands or while holding a sword.

- 🎚️ **Adjustable zoom**: starts at **×4**, scroll to zoom up to **×230**. While zooming, the mouse wheel zooms instead of switching hotbar slots.

- 🎬 **The real spyglass feel**: the same smooth zoom as the vanilla spyglass, a mouse sensitivity that slows down with the zoom, and the spyglass sounds. No black scope overlay, just a clean view (your hands are hidden while you look).

- 🧍 **Visible in third person**: in F5, your character raises a spyglass to their eye, on the ground and in the middle of an elytra flight.

- 🌐 **Multiplayer ready**: players who have the mod see you holding the spyglass up, and everyone nearby hears it. Singleplayer and LAN work out of the box.

- 🎭 **Animation mods friendly**: made to work with **Fresh Animations** (including the **Player** add-on), **Entity Model Features** and **Not Enough Animations**. Bonus: it also fixes the real spyglass with these mods, so the arm no longer drops when you turn around or fly.

## 📥 Installation

Get the [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com/app), search for **Spyglass** and click **Install**, that's it! You can also drop the `.jar` into your `mods` folder, next to **Fabric API**.

**Requirements:** Minecraft **26.3**, Fabric Loader **0.19.5** or newer and **Fabric API**.

**🌐 Playing on a server?** Install the mod on the server too, so players can see each other's spyglass animation. Without it, the zoom still works for you, only the animation isn't shared.

## 🎮 How to use

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Look through the spyglass | Hold **C** |
| Zoom in / out (×4 → ×230) | **Mouse wheel** while zooming |
| See your character hold it up | **F5** |

You can change the key anytime in **Options → Controls → Key Binds**.

## ❓ FAQ

**Do I need a spyglass in my inventory?**

No, the mod works without the item. A real spyglass keeps working normally too.

**Does it work on vanilla servers and Realms?**

Yes! The zoom is fully client-side. Only showing the animation to other players needs the mod on the server.

**The C key is already used.**

C is also vanilla's *Save Hotbar Activator* (creative mode) and a common key for zoom mods. Just rebind one of them in the controls.

**Will it change my worlds?**

No, it adds nothing to your worlds: you can remove it at any time and keep playing normally.
